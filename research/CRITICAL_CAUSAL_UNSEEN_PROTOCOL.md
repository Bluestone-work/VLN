# Cross-scene descriptive confirmation of critical-state interventions

Registered 2026-10-07 after the training census completed. The nine training
failures had SR rescue with native action replacement on 6/9 routes, including
4/9 without nDTW degradation; none of four first-event interrupt trials rescued
success. We now test the unchanged state/candidate/interrupt rule on all 22
failures in the already-inspected unseen66 diagnostic cohort.

This extends the training-only rollout scope of CRITICAL_CAUSAL_PROTOCOL.md.
All 22 routes remain in the denominator. Thresholds, checkpoint, seed 100, native
STOP, sensors and 15-decision horizon are unchanged. Exhaust all admissible graph
actions at the union of every execution-symptom state, last non-STOP and terminal
state. Use four first eligible ghost-segment cuts in episode-ID order with the
same sensing-only, consume-ghost and retain-ghost controls. Reproduce every
baseline route and audit every full-return prefix and metric before interpreting
results. Forced STOP at decision 14 is not disabled.

This cohort was previously used for diagnostic inspection. It is cross-scene
confirmation, NOT an untouched held-out benchmark or an independent test chosen
before all research. No validation outcomes may be used as training targets,
feature selection, threshold tuning or examples for fitting. Train/unseen
results must be reported separately. No aggregate rescued-oracle score should
be called deployable SR. No-rescue routes remain unresolved, not proposal failure.

Gate: unchanged from the training diagnostic. At least two routes in distinct
scenes with success rescue and no nDTW degradation permits only a registered
long-horizon graph preference investigation; strict majority requires more than
half of all baseline failures. Interrupt confirmation requires two rescued
routes/scenes versus both native and matched sensing-only controls. Four chosen
cuts cannot establish the upper bound for all timings, triggers or controllers.
